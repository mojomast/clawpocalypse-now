## Recognisable appearance (sources 2–3)

- **Silhouette:** a huge, voluminous mane of wavy, curly hair spreading wider than the shoulders. That big triangular hair mass is the defining silhouette. The build is lean and muscular, with bare arms.
- **Face and head:** a centre parting with face-framing strands. A strong, angular jaw with a stern brow and a sly half-smile. The face is fully visible between the hair curtains in both avatars. In the chibi avatar, round black sunglasses cover the eyes.
- **Hair:** copper or chestnut brown with warm orange highlights. Wavy to curly, with chunky locks. It falls well past the shoulders, to the chest in source 3.
- **Clothing:** a black leather vest with studs and no sleeves (source 2), or an open dark jacket (source 3). Several silver chains and a pendant. A small earring or hoop. Rings and a studded wristband.
- **Signature prop:** a black mug with a cyan **"TF"** logo and cyan steam.
- **Brand colours:** amber `#D4A855` and mint `#00D68F` from the TurboFit README and social art.

## Current game art vs sources

Compared against `assets/heroes/turbofit/*`, `assets/heroes/portraits/turbofit.png`, `after/turbofit.png` and `after/gameplay/turbofit.png`.

**Right:**
- Long brown hair, with the face visible and smiling under it, as Kyle asked.
- Black rock clothing, a spiked wristband and a lean, muscular build.
- The guitar-axe concept matches the community "TurboFit shreds guitar" idea.

**Wrong or unsourced:**
1. **Hair volume and texture.** The game hair is straight and lank, close to the head, and dark (`#301800`/`#603018`). The source is a huge wavy or curly mane in warmer copper (`#85320A`–`#D0894D`). This is the main silhouette miss.
2. **Top.** The game uses a black tank top. The source uses a **studded leather vest** with chains.
3. **Accessories.** The game has no chains, earring or sunglasses, and no TF mug.
4. **Tribal tattoo.** It is not in any source. Drop it or make it optional.
5. **Brand colours.** The game uses orange/red (`#ff9f43`, `#ffb02e` in `title.js`/`assist.js`). The brand pair is **amber `#D4A855` and mint `#00D68F`**, plus TF cyan.
