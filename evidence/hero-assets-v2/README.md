# Hero assets v2: Teknium, Nous Girl, Turbofit

This branch (`feat/natural-hero-assets`, stacked on PR #1 `fix/natural-hero-rig-animation`) replaces the
art and rig of **teknium, nous_girl and turbofit** with the approved v2 designs. It also adds the parts
the audit asked for: separate feet, separate hands, grip pairs, and secondary motion. **The four turtles
and don_piedro are untouched.** They have the same PNGs and the same `HERO_RIGS` rows, and their solved
matrices and rendered contact sheets are byte-identical (see "Untouched heroes" below).

`AUDIT.md` is the plan this implements (§1 scales, §3 integration, §4.2 checks, §5 proportions).
`before/` is the pre-change baseline at a53e191 (its own README explains how it was captured).

Runtime (`rig.js`, the new `secondary.js`, `foot-lock.js`): heroes listed in `HERO_V2` go through
`solveV2`. That path handles:

* ankle IK, with the foot rotated so the sole lies flat on `FOOT_Y`, and a toe-off fallback when the ankle can't reach;
* the wrist and hand chain, with the weapon parented to the hand;
* layering of grip_back, then the weapon, then grip_front;
* Turbofit's far-arm IK to the axe's second grip;
* a weapon floor guard (wrist first, then elbow);
* head/torso/hand variants driven by the pose, with 50 ms hysteresis;
* spring chains and the rope: fixed substeps, inertial frame, mirror-aware, dt=0 freezes, rope clamped above the floor.

All other heroes keep the v1 solve path unchanged. PR #1's collision, prop and depth-queue work and all
public IDs are preserved. The game is still build-free plain ES modules.

Source sheets were keyed by hue/chroma with despill, with connected-component checks that fail loudly.
They were sliced with `tools/slice-hero-parts.py <hero> --write` (Lanczos, antialiased alpha, AUDIT §1
scales). The `MANIFEST.md` tables and `hero-rigs.js` are generated from that (`tools/gen-hero-rigs.mjs`).
`hero-support.js` was regenerated by `tools/generate-support.mjs`. `src/SLICE.json` records each part's
sha256, and `tests/check-rig-sources.mjs` enforces it.

## Tests (all pass)

* `npm test`: rig sources (51 sliced parts match SLICE.json), rig geometry (2744 frames, 35300 attached joints, 2086 weapon grips), production draw, baseline manifest, **`tests/check-hero-v2.mjs`** (the new §4.2 checks), animation, world contacts, down/get-up, collision.
* `tests/check-hero-v2.mjs`:
  * every planted foot within 5° of flat (measured max 0.00°);
  * weapon hull never below `FOOT_Y + 2` (lowest 89.4 px);
  * grip point and axis coincide within 1 px / 1°, with grip_back < weapon < grip_front;
  * Turbofit's far hand within 2 px of grip2 in 30 two-hand frames;
  * variant pivots stay aligned, and head swaps are ≥ 50 ms apart at 30/60/144 Hz;
  * mirror-asymmetry flags are present;
  * springs agree within 3° across 30/60/144 Hz, with no NaN, ranges held, and a freeze at dt=0;
  * the rope stays finite and above the floor;
  * proportions are in bounds (teknium head 0.250 / arm 1.274 / leg 1.388; nous_girl 0.251 / 1.318 / 1.409; turbofit 0.248 / 1.329 / 1.357);
  * all 51 v2 PNGs have antialiased edges and no magenta spill.
* `npm run test:pixels`: 0 disconnections (>1 px) and 0 unusual overlaps across all 8 heroes (2422 joint samples).
* `npm run test:browser`: all 8 heroes play by keyboard with 0 console/render errors.
* `node tools/level-layout-smoke.mjs`: all eight stages render; the depth queue is intact.

## Known limits

* **The art is cut-out, not redrawn per pose.** Hands come in four drawings (open, fist, grip_back, grip_front; plus grip_far for Turbofit). Strong foreshortening, such as a fist pointing at the camera, still uses the side-view drawing.
* **Nous Girl's cable hangs nearly straight in idle and walk.** It is a light rope under gravity. It only visibly lashes on whip hits, and it is clamped at the floor rather than colliding with props.
* **Toe-off.** World-contact sampling records 22 toe-off support frames in fast runs. In those, the leg can't reach a flat-foot ankle target, so the foot pivots on the toe. The planted-foot flatness check counts these and excludes them.
* **The weapon floor guard bends the wrist, then the elbow.** It overrides the authored wrist in 8 of 118 Teknium poses
  (up to 34°, e.g. healthSpecial_active, grabThrow_active) and 16 of 122 Turbofit poses (up to 56°, e.g. grabThrow_active,
  bash/pound follow-through, turn). The final wrist always stays inside the MANIFEST `Wrist:` window. Nous Girl's
  short plug never needs it. Low weapon swings therefore read a little flatter than authored.
* **Spring and rope state lives per actor in the draw path.** The `render-rigs` contact sheets are static rest poses, so they show springs at rest. The motion strips and the real game show the dynamics.
* **Portraits are unchanged.** The HUD portraits in `assets/heroes/portraits/` were out of scope. Nous Girl's HUD portrait still shows the old blue-haired design.
* **One harness 404.** A single unattributed HTTP 404 console message (also present in the baseline, see `before/README.md`) appears in the motion capture. No rig image is missing: the harness throws on any failed part load.
