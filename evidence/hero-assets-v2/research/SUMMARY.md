## Teknium: CONFIRMED
An anime young man in 3/4 view with his **eyes always hidden behind an oversized white wedge visor carrying two diagonal red stripes** (`#FDFDFD`/`#C24E4E`). His **messy, spiky sage-teal hair** (`#72AD97`, shadow `#3A685E`) turns into a **cluster of bright green leaves** on the back and crown (`#196B1F`/`#0F6014`). He wears a **forest-green zip jacket with a tall funnel collar** (`#3C6847`/`#1F372A`) and has pale skin (`#E9D1C5`); a small wing motif (🪽) is optional. The cyan energy blade, trousers and sneakers are game equipment and costume. Current art gets the green spiky hair, red eyewear and green outerwear roughly right. It misses the visor shape (orange goggles instead of a white wedge with red stripes), the leaves, the hair colour (emerald instead of sage-teal), the jacket (a trench with invented red/white lining) and the skin tone (tanned instead of pale). Use no photos. The avatar's rights are unknown, so use it as reference only.

## Nous Girl: CONFIRMED
A retro 70s/80s shoujo-manga girl drawn in **pure black ink on white**. She has a **solid black bob with blunt straight bangs and outward-flipped ends at the shoulders**, **white headphones** (a band over the crown plus over-ear cups), large lashed eyes, a small nose, slightly parted lips and a calm, attentive expression. She wears a **simple collared shirt or blouse**. The only colour accent is **Nous electric blue `#0000F2`**, with the booklet's "Blues of Nous" (e.g. `#00769A`, `#003D59`, `#A4C4DD`, `#000F26`) available for shading and FX. The audio-cable whip and bass-drop are game equipment. Current art gets the bob, bangs, white band and manga face right. It is wrong on the **blue hair** (official hair is black), the **missing ear cups**, the invented **sailor uniform**, the short helmet-like bob (it should flare at the shoulders) and the soft full-colour shading (the official look is hard ink). The art is © Nous Research with no public licence: draw an original version, do not trace or ship the badge, stamp or wordmark, and get permission if the game is commercial.

## TurboFit: PARTIAL
Publicly, TurboFit is **sovthpaw's (GitHub SouthpawIN, X @vSouthvPawv) local-inference provider for Hermes Agent**. The game's own lines quote it ("TurboFit Check… we fit"). His creator-controlled avatar is a **rocker with a huge wavy copper-brown mane** (`#350800`/`#85320A`/`#A84816`/`#D0894D`) and a visible face with a stern brow and smirk. He wears **black sunglasses, a studded black leather vest, layered silver chains, an earring and a studded wristband**, and sips from a **black "TF" mug with cyan steam** (`#64D9CF`). The project's brand colours are **amber `#D4A855` and mint `#00D68F`**. The guitar-axe and shockwave are game equipment, and a community Hermes fighting game by @123mikeyd, which sovthpaw endorsed, also gives TurboFit a guitar. Current art gets the long brown hair, visible face, black rock look and guitar right. Its hair is straight and lank rather than a big wavy mane, it uses a tank top instead of a studded vest, the tribal tattoo has no source, and it lacks the chains, TF mug and amber/mint accents. **No link between TurboFit and Kyle (mojomast, ussy.host) was found.**
